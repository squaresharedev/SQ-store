## Prioritised gaps

**P0** (applies today under the DSA, or blocks switching Stripe Connect on)

1. **No seller freeze (R2).** Nothing per seller stops checkout; a ban leaves every listing and checkout live; an already-open Stripe page stays payable for 31 minutes after a takedown. Add `seller_restrictions`, read it in `gatePurchasableLines` and `placeStripeOrder`, an Admin freeze action, and expire open sessions on freeze or takedown. Schema change.
2. **No path to inform Stripe and no tamper-proof log (A2, L1).** "Stripe informed" has nowhere to live; decisions are deletable by service_role and are cascade-deleted with the seller's account. Add the append-only `moderation_events` table with a no-update/no-delete trigger, fix the `moderation_decisions` FK, and write a short inform-Stripe runbook. Schema change.
3. **Art. 16 reporter notices (S4).** No receipt and no decision notice to a reporter, although the form promises a confirmation. No intake for notices sent by email. No schema change needed for receipt and decision mail (needs mail on).
4. **Art. 18 (S6).** No process at all. Runbook plus an Admin escalation action.
5. **Statements of reasons with holes (R3/S5).** A ban tells the user nothing; an automated pause is never emailed; staff notices email only if Admin's Brevo secrets exist (unverified). Store mail is off, which also keeps the Terms with the moderation section and contact point (S1 to S3) from taking effect for existing sellers.

6. Early fraud warnings and disputes into a per-seller signal and ops alert (D3).
7. Admin application-fee refund (R5) and verified disconnect mechanics for Express accounts (R4).
8. A visible Art. 11/12 contact-point page with one consistent mailbox (S1, S2).
9. 24-hour target made visible in the Admin queue, with overdue re-alerts (O1).
10. Monthly director summary (O2), after L1 and R2 exist.
11. Terms agreement enforced server-side at publish, and the AUP cross-checked against Stripe's restricted list in a new Terms version (P1).
12. Category at onboarding with high-risk review, and a new-seller hold (P2, D2): owner decisions.
